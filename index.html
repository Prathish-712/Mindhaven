Need immediate coping strategies right now?

🫁 Deep Breathing

Breathe in slowly for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Repeat.

❄️ Ice Water Shock

Splash cold water on your face or hold ice cubes to quickly trigger the vagal calming reflex.

🚶 Mindful Movement

Take a slow 5-minute walk, do gentle shoulder rolls, or stretch to release muscular tension.
